GO LIVE

App publishing

Plan the steps to submit your app to official stores.

A

Apple App Store

  1. Prepare an app build that meets Apple's requirements.
  2. Enroll in the Apple Developer Program.
  3. Prepare screenshots, description, privacy details, and age rating.
  4. Upload the build through Apple's supported publishing tools.
  5. Submit for review and address any feedback.
Official Apple App Store resources ↗
▶

Google Play

  1. Prepare a signed Android App Bundle.
  2. Create a Google Play Console developer account.
  3. Complete the store listing and policy declarations.
  4. Test with the required testing tracks for your account.
  5. Submit the release for review.
Official Google Play Console ↗
ⓘ
Publishing is not automatic yet

This workspace currently provides guidance only. App signing, accounts, compliance, builds, and store submissions still need to be configured.
